# Psoydo Insights

Eight original German articles and a static, categorized hub at /de/insights.html. All content is readable without JavaScript. Author attribution names wescaleIT as editorial provider, not developer or IP owner.

## Editorial rules
- Keep each article focused on its own reader question; no keyword variants without distinct value.
- Scenarios are explicitly invented, not customer evidence.
- Distinguish practical recommendations from verified Psoydo capabilities. No connector, accuracy, compliance or infrastructure promises.
- Legal definitions link to primary sources. Review legal content when its source changes.
- Keep visible text, metadata, article relationships and sitemap synchronized.
- Article publication dates are deliberately omitted until publication, rather than inventing history.
- The inquiry CTA leads to personal qualification; no checkout or automatic provisioning.

## Maintenance
The HTML files are the authoritative editable content. Maintain navigation and footer consistently across all public pages. Static QA and browser captures include the new hub and articles.
